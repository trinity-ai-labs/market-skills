# Business plan — example product

`--binding-driver`'s evidence rule, section side. Both verdicts here state a pair
the closure holds, so the ledger is honest in both cases — and only one of the two
renders it, which is exactly the gap a disjunction between the note and the
section could never see.

## Target & verdict {#target-verdict}

The account count does not clear at the terms both channels set.
Evidence: 2 sources, 2 counterparties.

The margin does not clear at the fee the platform charges. Nothing here says what
that finding rests on, which is the planted failure: the note behind it knows the
answer is one source from one party, and a reader of this section cannot tell it
from a finding resting on twenty deals across twelve parties.

Deliberately no line for that corner and no em dash either — an em dash is what a
well-evidenced corner carries, and this one is not well evidenced.
