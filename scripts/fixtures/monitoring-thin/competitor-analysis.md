# Competitor Analysis — example product

The axis table is here and two of its three axes are incomplete. The third is
complete on purpose: firing is only half of what needs asserting, and an axis
that names all three columns has to stay silent or the check reports every
correct row as a gap.

## Threat ranking

| Threat | Impact | Probability | Confidence | Scenario |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| The incumbent bundles the capability into its existing tier | High | Medium | M | Bundled at no extra charge |

Outside the monitoring section, so its rows are not axes. A mode that read every
table in the document would report this row's first cell as an axis with no
instrument, no cadence and no decision.

Row two names no instrument, so it is a thing somebody intends to notice rather

rule and the reason the test is letter-or-digit rather than a list of the
spellings of `TBD`.
