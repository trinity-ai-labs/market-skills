The ceiling half of the asymmetry, and the note that has to stay silent. It
carries none of the five verdict fields, and `steady-state-ceiling` is
`required: true` and predates them — so every existing vault holds a claim
shaped exactly like this one, and a rule that owed the fields here would fail
every one of them on the day the skill updates. Carrying any one of the five
would make the rest owed; carrying none owes none.

Its section renders at the `{#steady-state}` anchor with this note behind it,
and there is deliberately no `{#steady-state}` equivalent of `verdict-unfiled`:
a rendered ceiling section with no field-carrying note behind it is the
legitimate case, which is the same asymmetry one document over.
