# Fixture plan

## What this vault does not say

This plan takes no position on what to charge. The words below exist to prove
the boundary rule rather than to say anything: pricing is not settled, the
prices competitors publish are not an anchor this plan recommends, and a
comparison that reads as priceless is one nobody can act on.

A substring scan finds the alias inside every one of those three words and
reports a subject this corpus never raises. A token scan reads `pricing`,
`prices` and `priceless` as single tokens, none of them the alias, and stays
silent - which is what the failing fixture beside this one proves from the other
direction.
