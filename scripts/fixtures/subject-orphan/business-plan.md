# Fixture plan

## The phase that opens this segment

The second phase of the schedule lands next year, and the growth rate the
segment has run at since the first phase is what carries the projection. No note
in this vault is filed under the subject that alias belongs to, so the plan is
arguing from a position the ledger has never held.

## What is deliberately absent

Nothing in this vault names the highest-ranked way the product dies, under its
key or under any alias of it. A subject the corpus has nothing to say about is
not a gap, and a mode that reported it would be coverage-gap over every optional
term - which fails a vault for declaring a vocabulary richer than the position
it took.

## The half coverage-gap already owns

The steady state ceiling this configuration converges on is named here and filed
nowhere, and this mode says nothing about it: the subject is `required: true`,
so the note is owed whether or not any document mentions it, and coverage-gap in
the bare `check` is the one that asks.
