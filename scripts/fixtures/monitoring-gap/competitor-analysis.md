# Competitor Analysis — example product

A rendered document at the vault root, like `business-plan.md` and `red-team.md`.
`--monitoring` reads one thing in it and nothing else: the axis table under the
`## Monitoring plan` heading.

This document has no such heading. That is the dodge the check has to close —
with the failure written as *the section carries no axes*, the cheapest way past
it is to delete the section, and then a document that never said which way any
competitor is moving reads exactly like one whose axes are all complete.

## Threat ranking

| Threat | Impact | Probability | Confidence | Scenario |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| The incumbent bundles the capability into its existing tier | High | Medium | M | Bundled at no extra charge, which removes the price wedge |
| A second entrant funds a direct copy | Medium | Low | L | Watch, not act — one data point |

The table above is here to prove the section boundary holds. Its first cell is
free prose and its rows are pipe rows, so a mode that read every table in the
document would report two axes here, neither of them named by anybody as
something to watch.

## Coverage

Two competitors profiled, no monitoring plan written. Which is the state this
fixture exists to fail.
