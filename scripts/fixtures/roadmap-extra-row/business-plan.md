# Business plan — example product

The forward direction of `--roadmap-table`: a roadmap table carrying a row that
matches no `milestone` note title. The item escaped the ledger, so it moves no
assumption anybody can name and the model behind it carries a dated change with
nothing under it.

Both other rows match their notes verbatim, which is what makes the failure
count the assertion with teeth: a check that reported every row would fire three
times here and still pass a census that only looks for the check name.
