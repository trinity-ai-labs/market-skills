A TENTH WORD. The enumeration is closed at nine, unquoted, the way `sensitivity`,
`driver_kind` and `model_input` are — and `valuation` is a plausible-looking value
that is not one of them, which is the whole point: the class is what SETS the
shelf life, so an unrecognised value is a fact whose window nobody can derive
while it reads as classified.

`stale_after` here is `"permanent"` so this note is reported for its class alone.
That isolation is deliberate: a fixture that tripped two rules at once would still
be green with either of them deleted.

The rule fires on a tenth word and never on the WRONG one of the nine, and that is
the design rather than a gap. A positioning claim filed as `description` takes
twelve months of shelf life instead of six and sits stale for the extra half-year
with nothing able to report it — but a check that inferred the class from the
sentence would cry wolf, and a check that cries wolf gets switched off.
