# Business plan — example product

The section-boundary half of `--binding-driver`. The verdict anchor opens a `###`
subsection one line into its body, and everything this mode reads sits inside it:
the corner verdict table, both conditional phrases and the evidence line.

Read to the next heading of ANY depth, all three fall outside the anchor's body —
the mode finds zero corner rows, so the `Kind` check never runs in either
direction, and the two condition checks and the two evidence checks cry wolf over
strings the reader can see. Read to the next heading of the same depth or
shallower, this vault carries exactly one failure.

## Target & verdict {#target-verdict}

Two corners do not clear, and each one is solved below.

### How each corner was solved

The outlet count does not clear at six hours a week across two channels, and the
margin does not clear at the price point you named. Both are decisions rather
than category floors, which is what makes each verdict conditional.

| Corner | Verdict | Binding driver | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| low value · early date | does not clear at six hours a week across two channels | reach | policy |
| high value · early date | does not clear at the price point you named | price | structural |
| low value · late date | clears | — | — |

The second row is the planted one: its `Kind` cell reads `structural` and the note
behind it carries `policy`, so the plan reports a decision the founder made as a
category floor. The first row matches its note and the third is a corner where
nothing binds — an em dash in both cells, owing no kind and no note.

Both corners rest on the one source note this vault carries, so each owes the line
the rule generates off its own counts. Evidence: 1 source, 1 counterparty — stated
once, because both corners have the same tail, and it keeps this vault to the one
failure it was built for.
