# Business plan — example product

The forward direction of the `Kind` column check: a cell that disagrees with the
note behind its corner. The column renders off `driver_kind`, so the two cannot
drift unless the cell was edited by hand.

## Target & verdict {#target-verdict}

Two corners do not clear and one does. Each row names the driver that binds and
that driver's kind, and each of those two cells is matched verbatim against the
note behind the corner.

| Corner | Verdict | Binding driver | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| low value · early date | does not clear at six hours a week across two channels | reach | structural |
| high value · early date | does not clear at the price point you named | price | policy |
| low value · late date | clears | — | — |

The first row is the planted one. The second matches its note, and the third is a
corner where nothing binds — an em dash in both cells, owing no kind and no note,
which is what the check has to skip rather than report as a corner with nothing
behind it.

Both corners rest on the one source note this vault carries, so each owes the line
the rule generates off its own counts. Evidence: 1 source, 1 counterparty — stated
once, because both corners have the same tail, and it keeps this vault to the one
failure it was built for.
