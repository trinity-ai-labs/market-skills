row of the corner table names. The table stops being a rendering of the set the

## Target & verdict {#target-verdict}

One corner does not clear and one does, and the retention corner is missing —
which is the planted failure, not a shape the template allows.

| Corner | Verdict | Binding driver | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| low value · early date | does not clear at six hours a week across two channels | reach | policy |
| high value · late date | undetermined — flips across the multiple's band | multiple | — |
| low value · late date | clears at the position inside the band this product sits at | — | — |

The second row is the shape `plan-template.md` writes for an undetermined corner:
its driver is named and its `Kind` cell is an em dash. Nothing in this vault
carries `multiple` as a binding driver, so it demands no note — and a rule that
required a kind wherever a driver was named would report this row on every plan
written to the template.

Both corners rest on the one source note this vault carries, so each owes the line
the rule generates off its own counts. Evidence: 1 source, 1 counterparty — stated
once, because both corners have the same tail, and it keeps this vault to the one
failure it was built for.
