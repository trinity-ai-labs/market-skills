# Business plan — example product

`--binding-driver`'s condition check: a policy-bound verdict whose section states
the finding and drops the configuration it is conditional on. The string is
matched verbatim against the section `used_in` names, because the section is
supposed to render off the field.

## Target & verdict {#target-verdict}

The outlet count does not clear by the date you gave. Reach binds.

The margin does not clear at the category conversion benchmark, and conversion is
structural — nothing about the configuration is stopping that one, which is why
the note behind it carries no condition and this section owes none.

There is deliberately no corner verdict table here. The `Kind` column check needs
one, so leaving it out is what keeps this vault to a single failure.
