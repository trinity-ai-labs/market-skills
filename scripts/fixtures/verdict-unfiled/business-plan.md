# Business plan — example product

A rendered verdict with nothing in the ledger behind it — `--roadmap-table`
inverted. That mode fails milestone notes with no plan to render them; this fails
a plan section with no note behind it. The vault carries no notes at all, which is
the point: the section is the whole trigger.

## Target & verdict {#target-verdict}

The outlet count does not clear by the date you gave. Reach binds, and at six
hours a week across two channels the count lands around a third of the way.

Every property the ledger would have given that sentence is missing: no
`rests_on`, so no confidence derivation and no cap; no `stale_after`, so nothing
ever comes up for re-checking; no supersession when the target is renegotiated, so
the superseded finding is simply overwritten. What triggers the check is this
section being here and non-empty, never a reading of these words.

## Steady state {#steady-state}

Non-empty too, and deliberately not a failure. There is no `{#steady-state}`
equivalent of the check above, because a ceiling section in an existing plan
legitimately has no field-carrying note behind it — the same asymmetry one
document over. A mirror rule here would fail every plan written before the fields
existed.
