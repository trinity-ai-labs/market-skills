# Business plan — example product

The success line `--binding-driver` prints when the ledger carries a verdict and
the plan carries no corner table for it to be read against. Nothing here fails,
and that is the point: what is asserted is that the line says the `Kind` check did
not run, rather than reporting that the ledger and the table agreed.

Zero rows compared and zero rows disagreeing are the same word in a line that ends
`matched verbatim`, and they are not the same answer — which is what let a
section-boundary bug ship as a clean pass over a table nothing ever opened.

## Target & verdict {#target-verdict}

The outlet count does not clear by the date you gave. Reach binds, and the
category conversion benchmark is a floor the category sets rather than a
configuration anybody here can change — so the note behind this verdict carries
`driver_kind: structural` and owes no stated condition.

There is deliberately no corner verdict table under this anchor. That absence is
the whole fixture: with it, the mode has rows to compare and prints a count;
without it, the `Kind` check runs in neither direction and the line has to say so.

Evidence: 1 source, 1 counterparty.
